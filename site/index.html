Features

Everything a radio dial should be,
built from music you love.

Always on air

Every station runs on its own timeline, worked out from the clock. Tune in at any moment and you land exactly where the station is, just like real radio. Two devices tuned to the same station play the same song at the same time.

Stations from simple rules

Mix and match libraries, artists, albums, genres and decades. A live counter shows how many songs match as you go, and smart stations pick up new music automatically.

Three ways to play

Track Shuffle plays every song once before repeating any. Album Shuffle keeps albums whole. Straight plays a whole discography in release order, perfect for a deep dive.

Crossfades and gapless albums

Shuffle stations blend songs together with 2, 3, 5 or 8 second crossfades. Albums play without a gap between tracks, the way they were meant to be heard.

Made for the car

Your steering wheel's next and previous buttons switch stations like radio presets, or skip songs if you prefer. Lock screen controls and Bluetooth just work.

A TV-style guide

See what's on every station right now and what's coming up next. Click any song in the guide, past or future, to play it now, then jump straight back to live.

Works with your other players

Radiarr publishes its stations as a standard M3U playlist with an XMLTV guide, so you can add them to Jellyfin, Plex Live TV, Kodi, TiviMate or VLC. It can also cast to Google Cast speakers.

Yours, and private

Radiarr runs on your own computer or home server. Your music and your listening never leave your network, and stations use no processing power until someone tunes in.

Now Playing

Flip stations like a car radio.

Big artwork, the song and album at a glance, what's up next, and the stations either side one tap away. Don't like what's on? Skip the song, or change the channel and land mid-song on something else.

Station editor

“80s soft rock.” “Every Jimmy Eat World album, in order.” Done.

Name it, choose a few rules and watch the matching song count update live. Pick a thumbnail from the station's own album art, choose a badge colour, and it's on air.

Your lineup

Starter stations in seconds.

Radiarr looks at your library and suggests stations by genre, era and artist, so you have a full dial from the first minute. Reorder, duplicate, reshuffle or switch stations off, one at a time or in bulk.
